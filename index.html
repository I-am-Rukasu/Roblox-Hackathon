01 / What is Build Day?

EVERY EXPERIENCE
STARTS WITH AN IDEA.

Build Day is a free online development hackathon for emerging and experienced Roblox creators. Participants learn from industry practitioners, work independently or in teams, and publish a functional experience by the end of the program.
